# AetherLearn Android App Vision

## Overview
AetherLearn is an AI-powered study companion designed for students and lifelong learners. The platform combines adaptive quizzing, AI-generated flashcards, collaborative spaces, and gamified progression into a single mobile-first experience. This document outlines the goals, guiding principles, and feature scope for the initial release.

## Product Pillars
1. **Intelligent Study Automation** – Convert raw study materials into structured quizzes, flashcards, and summaries using on-device models augmented by cloud inference.
2. **Personalized Learning Journeys** – Adapt content difficulty, scheduling, and recommendations to each learner's habits and goals.
3. **Social & Motivational Layer** – Encourage long-term engagement through collaboration, community challenges, and achievement systems.
4. **Inclusive Mobile Experience** – Deliver a minimalist, accessible interface optimized for Android devices with optional desktop/tablet support.

## Target Personas
- **University Students** balancing multiple courses and needing rapid content digestion.
- **Professional Upskillers** preparing for certifications with limited study time.
- **Lifelong Learners** exploring new topics and valuing flexible, AI-guided assistance.

## Feature Highlights
- Custom Quiz Generator with adaptive difficulty.
- AI Flashcard Creator from images, documents, and typed notes.
- Bite-sized lesson feed with instant summaries.
- Insightful dashboards, streaks, and badges.
- Shared decks, group quizzes, and real-time annotations.
- Optional helpers: AI essay assistant, math solver, code debugger, research assistant.

## Guiding UX Tenets
- **Flow-Oriented Navigation**: Tabbed experience for Learn, Practice, Review, and Collaborate.
- **Glanceable Insights**: Visualize mastery using cards, charts, and progress rings.
- **Focus Mode**: Distraction-free sessions with ambient sounds and haptic reminders.
- **Accessibility First**: WCAG AA contrast, voice control, captions, and offline caching.

## Non-Goals (v1)
- Offline model training (server-side inference only in v1).
- Full desktop parity (responsive web planned later).
- Support for institution-level administration.

## Success Metrics
- Daily active learners completing at least one session.
- Retention rates for week 1 -> week 4 cohorts.
- NPS feedback on AI-generated content quality.
- Quiz completion rates and mastery improvements over time.

## Next Steps
- Finalize data schema for quizzes, flashcards, and user progress.
- Prototype adaptive algorithms with small pilot group.
- Build UI mockups and gather usability feedback.
- Integrate secure auth, storage, and analytics foundations.
